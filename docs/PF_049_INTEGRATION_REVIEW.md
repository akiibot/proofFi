## Corrected behavior

- Home mirrors `policy/engine.py`: eligible liquidity excludes high-risk assets;
  protection is max(goal obligation, active tuition reserve) plus other protected
  obligations plus emergency floor. With $17,000 eligible cash, $7,000 tuition,
  $16,000 active reserve and $2,000 floor it shows $18,000 protected and attention.
  This remains display arithmetic; only the backend determines action eligibility.
- Activity and Story share one execution evidence reader. A successful historical
  receipt or claim cannot imply confirmation. A complete backend Execution
  response is read through the authenticated read-only endpoint, then authenticated by its canonical digest
  against the **latest** execution artifact in the browser-verified audit chain.
  Pending, confirmed, failed and reorged observations retain their backend status.
  A later reconciliation or ledger reversal invalidates older observations.
  Missing/unavailable/mismatched evidence or broken audit yields unknown, never confirmation.
  A display-only cache supplies a conservative fallback during mixed-version rollout.
- Ledger audit records are joined by execution_id, including those without a
  certificate_id, and are included in the Story export. No receipt inference or
  alternate financial authority is introduced.
- Consent explicitly authorizes approval **and sending** and is bound to the
  displayed certificate ID. Approval success is followed by fresh verification
  and hash-checked audit reads; independent backend execution checks remain.
- Pending/reserved copy does not assert broadcast. Reorged and confirmed screens
  retain reconciliation. Story links back to the original attempt. Reconciliation
  uses the retained request identity, never a new signature or dispatch request.
- Reopened drawers/dialogs focus after their portal mounts, trap Tab and restore
  focus on Escape. Ask does not label unverified alternative planner proposals
  safe, or invent a path count before simulation responds.
